# Brain-Flip
 
A fast, loud, couch-friendly party game. One screen (the TV) shows the action; everyone else plays on their phone with two giant buttons: **TRUE** and **FALSE**.
 
The twist: **the rule keeps changing.** A word appears on the TV, and you have just over two seconds to answer a question about it — but the question flips every few turns, so you can never get comfortable.
 
---
 
## How to Play
 
### The Goal
Be the **last player standing**, or have the **highest score** when 20 prompts have been played.
 
### Each Round
1. The TV shows a word (like *Penguin* or *Skateboard*) and reads it aloud.
2. A timer bar drains. You have **2.25 seconds**.
3. Tap **TRUE** if the word fits the **active rule**, **FALSE** if it doesn't.
### The Rules (they rotate every 5 prompts)
 
| Rule | Tap TRUE if… |
|---|---|
| 🦅 **DOES IT FLY?** | the thing flies |
| 🐬 **DOES IT SWIM?** | the thing swims |
| 🚲 **HAS WHEELS?** | the thing has wheels |
 
When the rule changes, the TV flashes a full-screen banner, sounds a siren, and a voice announces it:
*"New rule: does it swim?"*
 
Across a 20-prompt game, you'll see four rule blocks, and the rule never repeats back-to-back.
 
### Example
With the rule **DOES IT SWIM?**
 
| Word | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dolphin | TRUE | Swims |
| Cat | FALSE | Doesn't |
| Submarine | TRUE | Vehicles count |
| Penguin | TRUE | Can't fly, but swims great |
 
Switch the rule to **DOES IT FLY?** and *Penguin* is suddenly FALSE. That's the whole game.
 
### Lives & Scoring
 
| Event | Result |
|---|---|
| ✅ Correct answer | **+100** points |
| ⚡ Correct in **under 1 second** | **+50** bonus (150 total) |
| ❌ Wrong answer | **−1 ❤️** |
| ⏰ No answer in time | **−1 ❤️** |
 
Everyone starts with **3 hearts**. Lose them all and your phone shows **ELIMINATED** — you can still watch and heckle.
 
### When the Game Ends
- **Multiplayer:** the game ends the moment only one player is left alive, or after 20 prompts. If it goes the distance, the **highest score among survivors** wins (ties are shared).
- **Solo:** the game ends when you run out of hearts or finish all 20 prompts.
- If the last players are knocked out on the same prompt, the one with the higher score wins.
After the finale (confetti and a fanfare, naturally), the host can start another round with the same players.

## Your Phone
 
- **Top bar:** your name, remaining hearts, and score.
- **Rule label:** the current rule is always shown above the buttons, so you never forget what you're answering.
- **Buttons:** big and bright — **green TRUE** on top, **red FALSE** below. They only light up while a prompt is live, and lock the moment you answer (no changing your mind).
- **Feedback:** after each prompt your phone flashes green or red with your points, and vibrates (if your device supports it).
- **Disconnected?** Reload the page and you'll rejoin as the same player, with your hearts and score intact.
- **Joined late?** You'll sit out the current game and be dealt in on the next one.
On a computer, you can also play with the keyboard: **T** / **←** for TRUE, **F** / **→** for FALSE.

## The TV Screen
 
- A **rule bar** at the top with the active rule and five dots showing progress through the current rule block.
- The **word** in giant type, and a color-shifting timer bar that goes from green to red.
- A **scoreboard** at the bottom with every player's name, hearts, and score, plus little pop-ups for points won and hearts lost.
- **Screen shake and a red flash** whenever someone gets one wrong.
- A **winner screen** with final standings.

| Key | Action |

## Tips
 
- **Sound and voice on.** The voice reads every word and announces each rule change, and it helps a lot. Turn the TV volume up.
- **Don't answer the word — answer the rule.** Most mistakes happen right after a rule switch, when your brain is still on the old question.
- **Speed matters.** Under one second is worth 50% extra. If everyone's already answered, the round ends early, so the pace picks up fast.
- **Playing with kids?** The word list is family-friendly, and the trick questions make great "wait, really?" moments.
- **Big group?** Eight players fit on the scoreboard, but it's loud and chaotic in the best way.

## FAQ
 
**Is the timer really 2.25 seconds?**
Yes. The bar on the TV runs for exactly 2.25 seconds. Your phone times your tap from the moment the prompt reaches *your* device, so a slow connection doesn't cost you points.
 
**Can I change my answer?**
No. Your first tap locks in.
 
**What if nobody answers?**
Everyone who didn't answer loses a heart.
 
**Can two players have the same name?**
If they do, the second one gets a number added (e.g., "ANA 2").
 
**Is there a limit to how many times the same word shows up?**
Words don't repeat within a game until the list for that rule runs low, so a 20-prompt game will feel fresh.
 
**What if the TV stops responding?**
Phones show *Waiting for host…* and reconnect automatically if the TV comes back. If the TV tab is closed, the room ends.
